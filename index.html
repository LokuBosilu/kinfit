Scenarios

Three Situations Kinfit Is Built For

These are illustrative scenarios drawn from our research, not customer results. We are recruiting for a pilot to measure what actually happens.

Regular exercise is recommended for adult health (WHO, 2020), and it is a core treatment for knee and hip osteoarthritis (NICE, 2022; Bannuru et al., 2019). In Sri Lanka, non-communicable diseases account for 83% of deaths (WHO, 2018). Yet when you train without a professional present, nobody can tell whether your form is right or whether today is a day to push or a day to stop.

D

Dilani, the solo gym member

The blind spot. Dilani gets a plan from her trainer but trains alone on busy evenings. Her knee caves inward on every squat and nobody sees it. Errors that go unnoticed get repeated until they become habit.

How Kinfit responds. Her phone tracks her movement as she lifts and speaks a cue mid-set, such as “Knees out!”, so she can correct the very next rep.

What the pilot will measure. Of the sets where a cue fired, how many visibly improved within the same set.

N

Nimal, exercising for knee osteoarthritis at home

The blind spot. Nimal’s doctor told him to stay active, so he exercises at home with no supervision. He cannot see that his range has been shrinking. After a painful flare he stops exercising altogether.

How Kinfit responds. A short check-in comes before the session. During the set, Kinfit watches for slowing down, lost range and mid-rep pauses. If movement looks unsafe it stops the set and asks how he feels. His exercises are assigned by his trainer or clinician.

What the pilot will measure. How often flags are judged false on review (our target is under about 25 to 30%), and whether people keep to their plan over four weeks.

T

The trainer with too many clients

The blind spot. A trainer sees perhaps twenty to forty minutes of a client’s week. The rest is unobserved, and reviewing video afterwards is slow and arrives too late to change the session it describes.

How Kinfit responds. Instead of full workout recordings, the trainer opens a web portal with a short, prioritised queue of flagged moments, each with instant replay. Clips are shared only with the user’s consent. Trainers also use the portal to manage clients and assign plans.

What the pilot will measure. Whether flagged sets are actually reviewed. Our target is most of them within 24 hours.

What a trainer told us

In our first trainer interview, a local gym trainer who also runs his own gym said he looks at three things in a client’s session: is the movement correct, is rest time in range, and do the reps hold up at the prescribed weight. For clients with joint pain, he asks for the doctor’s report before he designs a plan. Kinfit’s supervised mode follows the same principle: it runs only exercises assigned by a trainer or clinician.

How it works

Three Things Kinfit Does

1. Live form correction

Prop your phone up and train. Kinfit counts your reps and corrects your form while the set is running, on screen and by voice. The live camera analysis runs on your phone.

2. Safety check and auto-stop

Before each session you complete a short readiness check-in. During the set, Kinfit watches for unsafe movement and signs of deterioration: slowing down, lost range of motion, mid-rep pauses. If movement becomes unsafe, it stops the set and asks how you feel. Kinfit asks. It does not diagnose.

3. Error-only trainer portal

Trainers do not watch full workout recordings. The web portal lets them manage clients, assign plans, and review a short, prioritised queue of flagged moments with instant replay, shared only with the user’s consent.

Modes

One App, Adapted to Your Supervision Level

Mode A: Active gym member

Follow a plan your trainer has designed, or start a Quick Workout on your own. You get live rep counting and real-time voice and on-screen corrections.

Mode B: Supervised exercise

For people with a condition such as osteoarthritis who exercise under professional supervision. The app runs only exercises assigned by a trainer or clinician, and asks for a stricter check-in before any movement begins.

Sample plan structure

Plans can follow a simple structure: warm-up, joint warm-up, modified strength work, then mobility. Your trainer or clinician decides the exercises.

Where Kinfit fits

What Exists Today, and the Gap We Target

From our review of existing products:

Existing product types and the gap Kinfit targets
Type of productWhat it does wellThe gap
Consumer fitness appsCount reps, estimate calories.Assume a healthy user. No notion of a condition, a flare or a safety limit.
Camera form coachesMeasure and correct form.Do not track deterioration, and sell to individuals rather than to a gym and its trainers.
Digital physiotherapy servicesCondition-specific exercise with clinicians attached.Typically insurer- or employer-funded and priced for Western markets. Built as remote physiotherapy, not supervision of someone who also trains at a gym.
Symptom and flare trackersRecord flares, medication and symptoms.Do not watch movement, so the signal arrives only once you already feel it.

Kinfit combines live form correction, a safety stop during the set, deterioration signals, and a flagged-moment queue for a real trainer.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my video leave my phone?

The live camera analysis runs on your phone. If a moment is flagged, a short clip of that moment can be shared with your trainer, and only with your consent.

Is Kinfit a medical device?

No. Kinfit is an exercise movement checker and coaching aid. It does not diagnose, give treatment advice or measure vital signs.

Can I use it without a trainer?

Yes, in Mode A you can start a Quick Workout on your own. Mode B runs only exercises assigned by a trainer or clinician.

Does it replace my trainer or doctor?

No. It hands decisions back to the people accountable for your plan.

What happens if Kinfit stops my set?

It prompts you and asks how you feel, and it can notify your trainer. It does not call for help. Kinfit is not an emergency service.

Which languages and phones?

The pilot is in English. Kinfit is being built for Android and iPhone.
